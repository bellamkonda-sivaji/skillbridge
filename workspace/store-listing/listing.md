### Data safety (what the apps collect)
- **Personal info:** name, phone number, email (optional), date of birth, gender — for the account and matching
- **Location:** approximate and precise — to show nearby work / nearby workers
- **Photos:** profile photo, business documents (employer verification) — uploaded by the user
- **Financial info:** bank account / UPI ID for worker payouts; payments handled by Razorpay
- **App activity:** applications, attendance check-in/out
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS); users can request account deletion.
- No data sold; no ads.

### Content rating
Questionnaire answers: no violence, no sexual content, no gambling, no user-to-user chat with strangers outside hiring context (in-app messages are between a business and an applicant), shares location (for nearby jobs).

### App access (for Google's review)
Login required. Provide a test worker and a test employer account on the live server in Play Console → App content → App access.
